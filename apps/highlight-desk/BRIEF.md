# Highlight desk

Status: not started. Example brief only.

Operator action: paste a long-form video link.

Machine steps, in order: fetch the source, find candidate moments, cut and reframe, add captions, match the cut to short-form references, render. A scout can list trending long and short videos as reference. It does not post.

Human gate: approve the post to a social account. The release handoff uses `needs-human` for that step.
